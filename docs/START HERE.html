Open & play

  1. Extract the entire triptotropicNEWo69.zip before opening it.
  2. Open OPEN TRIPTOTROPIC.cmd, or App → Triptotropic.exe. Your desktop icon opens the same app.
  3. Use the app's World menu to jump to Home, Assets Room, Brain World, or the Crystal Library.

This is a standalone desktop window with no browser tab or address bar. It uses Electron's bundled Chromium renderer internally. No internet or separately installed browser is needed to play. Keep the entire folder together when moving it.

Close the app before copying or backing up its Data folder. That folder holds the local database and saved browser settings. Cloud data and online accounts are separate.

One shelf for all the weird stuff

Open the orange Spawn shit button. Search or choose a category. The source label tells you which world a model came from. Original variants have separate stable names and IDs; image assets can be spawned as framed panels.

  1. Spawn a model.
  2. Open Physics. Choose it in the list, or enable Select models by clicking them and click the model.
  3. Choose a role, resize it, or enter X/Y/Z position and rotation.
  4. Use Save scene file to keep your arrangement. Load it in another shared scene with Load scene file.

WASD: move and turn. Space: jump. E: pick up/drop. F: throw/fire. Throws follow the model's facing direction, including toward the camera. Mobile players get a joystick and action buttons. Use Release control to return to the original scene camera.

Earth, Moon, zero and reverse gravity presets are available. Settings follow you between pages. Scene files preserve model IDs, roles, sizes, positions, rotations, and physics settings.

The thirteen gold altars

Enter the Crystal Library beside Bongo's lab. Walk near a book and click it, or open Customize → Read the 13 books. The reader works offline.

  1. The Lesser Key of Solomon: complete 1916 Goetia edition, with diagrams. This is the Goetia volume, not all five parts of the Lemegeton.
  2. Necronomicon shelf: an original companion and the full public-domain Call of Cthulhu. Lovecraft's fictional book has no authentic ancient full text; modern commercial versions are not included.
  3. The Book of Enoch: complete R. H. Charles 1917 translation.
  4. Illuminati: Robison's complete 1797 historical polemic, contextual notes, and two full open-access research articles about conspiracy beliefs. The articles do not establish modern Illuminati claims.
  5. MKULTRA: complete 173-page 1977 Senate hearing.
  6. WWII / Doctors' Trial: complete official Nuremberg Volume II, including the Medical Case judgments and Nuremberg Code.
  7. Programming: original AI-era workbook with foundations, exercises, debugging and a path to building your own 3D projects.
  8. Flying: complete FAA Airplane Flying Handbook, 406 pages. Use it with qualified instruction and the aircraft's approved handbook.
  9. Occult: complete 1922 abridged Golden Bough, plus a historical collection of charms and sorcery.
  10. Frankenstein.
  11. Alice in Wonderland.
  12. Flatland.
  13. On the Origin of Species: complete 1859 first edition.

Edition details, source links and retained licenses are inside the reader and book folders. Historical books may contain outdated language and claims.

What to know about this build

  • The world, models, downloaded books, local saves and menus work offline. Live AI, online social updates and external websites need internet. Offline Bongo uses local replies; it is not a downloaded language model.
  • Shared spawns use the same fixed-step physics in the main worlds. Original environment actors retain their specialized animation and interactions. The old HexTrip native game still uses its Rapier engine with shared gravity; its models are now also in the shared catalog. The future full Hex-tile editor merge remains a separate build.
  • Ragdolls use model parts and distance joints. Collisions use simple body bounds rather than exact per-triangle anatomy. Assigning Vehicle enables a general movement role, not a custom engine or wheel rig for every object.
  • The inspector controls spawned models. Existing scenery remains part of its original level; spawn its catalog version to resize or change its role.
  • There is a 150-object scene limit to keep the world usable. Large buildings and detailed living brains cost more than simple props.
  • No separate hidden Israel room source was found; the site contains a locked territory entry.
  • Scene arrangements are saved explicitly with Save scene file. Carry those JSON files and the Data folder to preserve your offline work.
