Ritual, Charms & the Golden Bough

Two complete historical collections of folklore, charms, ceremonies and beliefs. Their claims and terminology belong to their period.

The Golden Bough

James George Frazer. Complete 1922 abridged edition, with its account of ritual and comparative mythology.

Gypsy Sorcery and Fortune Telling

Charles Godfrey Leland, 1891. Complete illustrated historical collection of charms and folklore. The original title and dated language are retained in the source.

Both public-domain texts include their Project Gutenberg source and license.
