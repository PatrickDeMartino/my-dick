The Necronomicon: a fictional archive

The Necronomicon is a fictional book within H. P. Lovecraft’s stories. Its invented antiquity is part of the fiction. There is no single authentic ancient volume to reproduce. Several later authors created different commercial books with this title; those editions are not included here.

This altar instead holds the complete public-domain 1928 story The Call of Cthulhu, plus this original orientation. Read it as cosmic horror, not as historical evidence. Lovecraft often makes a narrative feel like an archive by presenting notes, reports, translations, and secondhand testimony. A convincing fictional document can be a powerful artistic device while remaining invented.
