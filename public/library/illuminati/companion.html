A research method

Record the exact claim, its date, the author, and the evidence offered. Distinguish an original letter or organizational record from someone’s later interpretation. Ask whether two accounts are independent or repeat the same source. Compare dates, names, and context. An absence of evidence does not establish a hidden cause.

Scientific studies of conspiracy beliefs examine questions such as how people evaluate evidence, perceive uncertainty, and form social identities. Such studies are not, by themselves, proof of a particular secret organization controlling events. Keep psychological research and historical claims in separate columns in your notes.

Your notebook

For each assertion, write: claim; primary source; publication date; direct quotation or paraphrase; alternative explanation; independent corroboration; unresolved uncertainty. Assign confidence only after checking the evidence. Preserve contradictory findings rather than deleting them to make a story feel cleaner.
